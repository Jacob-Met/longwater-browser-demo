Longwater: Fourteen Tides

Steward three marsh cells through fourteen tides. Use Tab to reach each control and Enter or Space to activate it. With a game control focused, 1, 2, 3 or the arrow keys select a cell. G opens its gate, H adds shade, S plants seeds, and R opens a review before starting a new watch. Read each cell's description for its current measurements and Field notes for the complete tide report.
